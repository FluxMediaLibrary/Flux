# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Flux contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, practical, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear and constructive.
- Assume reports are made in good faith.
- Keep criticism about code, behavior, docs, and project direction.
- Respect maintainers' time by providing reproduction steps, logs, and context when asking for help.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, personal attacks, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Repeated low-effort demands after maintainers have asked for more information.
- Sharing secrets, credentials, private media, or illegal content in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; block users; or take other reasonable action to keep the project healthy.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository owner's preferred contact path or GitHub security/advisory channels if the concern involves sensitive information.
